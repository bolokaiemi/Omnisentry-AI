ℹ️ About Omminsentiry AI

Advanced cybersecurity intelligence and real-time trust auditing

🎯 Our Mission

In an era where cyber threats, phishing scams, and domain spoofing are rising at exponential rates, Omminsentiry AI was built to bring transparency and advanced defensive intelligence to everyday internet browsing.

Our mission is to establish a global database of website safety, providing immediate trust auditing metrics using technical security checks and machine learning. We aim to shield users before credentials, personal data, or payment transactions are hijacked.

⚙️ Core Technology

The platform leverages several technically distinct security validation mechanisms to compile a domain risk assessment:

  • ✔ SSL Trust Verification: Audits certificate authenticity, encryption protocols, and checks for revocation.
  • ✔ WHOIS & Registrant Audit: Analyzes domain age, expiration dates, and registrars to identify high-risk throwaway domains.
  • ✔ Reputation Analytics: Uses lexical patterns to flag typosquatting, brand spoofing, and malicious intent.
  • ✔ Machine Learning Classification: Utilizes a Random Forest classifier model to output precise trust indicators based on multi-dimensional vectors.

📈 Beta Roadmap

We are currently in a public Beta testing cycle. As part of our pre-registration roadmap, we are validating our backend risk models and collecting tester reviews.

Once we record 5,000 active beta reviews and scans, the Omminsentiry AI platform will be fully registered as a security firm, enabling the release of browser extensions, router blocks, and live firewall threat feeds.
